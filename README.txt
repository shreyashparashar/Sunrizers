SUNRIZERS ENGINEERS - WEBSITE FILES
===================================

Open index.html in any browser to view the site.
To go live, upload ALL files and folders (css, js, images and every .html file)
to the public_html folder of your sunrizerssengineers.in hosting.

THINGS TO REPLACE BEFORE GOING LIVE
1. Phone numbers (+91-98XXX XXXXX), address and GSTIN: edit in every .html
   file (use Find and Replace in Notepad++ or VS Code across all files).
2. Team photos: images/team-1.jpg to team-4.jpg are stand-in stock photos.
   Replace them with real photos of your staff (same file names).
3. Google Map: in contact.html, replace the iframe src with your own
   Google Maps embed link.
4. Machinery list in infrastructure.html: edit to match your actual machines.
5. Enquiry forms use mailto: (opens the visitor's email app). For a proper
   form, connect a form service or a PHP mail script on your hosting.
