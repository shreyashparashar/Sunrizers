Infrastructure

Our workshop is set up for aluminium fabrication, MS fabrication, welding, machining and repair work. The shop floor has separate areas for cutting, welding, assembly, finishing and storage of material.

For site work we have our own portable welding sets, cutters, drills, grinders and scaffolding, so that installation and construction work does not get delayed.
